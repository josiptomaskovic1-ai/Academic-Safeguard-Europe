# Right of reply

Any institution with a published profile, and any national authority whose framework has been assessed, may publish a **statement of reply** alongside its findings, whether or not it also requests a correction.

## Format

- Up to 300 words.
- Plain text, with links to official documents allowed.
- Submitted from an official institutional email address or account, or through a GitHub issue that the institution confirms through an official channel.
- No personal data, and no comments about individual students or staff.

## Publication

- The statement is published unedited, except for removing personal data or unlawful content, with the date received.
- It appears on the profile page under "Statement of reply" and is stored in the record's `replies` field.
- ASE may add a short factual note, for example "Correction C-2026-004 accepted in response".

## Relationship with corrections

A statement of reply does not change a score by itself. If the statement identifies documents that ASE did not review, ASE opens a correction request on the institution's behalf and handles it under the [correction policy](correction-policy.md).

## Before publication

Before publishing a new institutional profile, ASE aims to notify the institution through a publicly listed contact address, so it can point out documents ASE missed. This is not yet systematic in v0.1; see the adversarial review.
