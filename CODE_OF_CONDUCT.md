# Code of conduct

Academic Safeguard Europe is a research project about accountability and fair procedure, and we apply the same values to ourselves.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith, including towards universities, academic staff and people who disagree with our findings.
- Argue from evidence. Cite sources and point to the rubric.
- Accept correction gracefully. Being wrong in public, and fixing it, is part of credible research.
- Respect people's languages and legal traditions. European systems differ, and difference is not failure.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- **Naming, accusing or discussing individuals** (staff, students or others) in connection with alleged misconduct, anywhere in the project: issues, pull requests, discussions or data.
- Publishing personal data, private correspondence, complaint files or anything that could identify a complainant or whistleblower.
- Using the project to pursue a personal dispute with an institution.
- Knowingly submitting invented or misrepresented sources.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content that breaks these rules, including content that contains personal data. They may also lock threads, and may temporarily or permanently exclude contributors. Serious breaches, especially the disclosure of personal data, are acted on immediately, without prior warning.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub's reporting tools ("Report content") or the contact address in the README once it is published. Please do not open a public issue for a conduct report.

This code is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
